THE LEGEND OF MEILE — Video Export Guide
=========================================

PREREQUISITES:
  - Godot 4.0+ (download: https://godotengine.org/download)
  - ffmpeg (for MP4 conversion)

=== OPTION A: Automatic (Linux/macOS) ===

  chmod +x export_video.sh
  ./export_video.sh                     # outputs meile_tribute.mp4
  ./export_video.sh my_video.mp4        # custom filename

=== OPTION B: Godot built-in movie writer (any OS) ===

  1. Open terminal / command prompt
  2. Run:

     godot --path /path/to/godot-tribute --write-movie meile_tribute.avi --fixed-fps 30

  3. The animation will play and auto-record to meile_tribute.avi
  4. Convert to MP4 with ffmpeg:

     ffmpeg -i meile_tribute.avi -c:v libx264 -crf 18 -pix_fmt yuv420p output.mp4

=== OPTION C: Frame-by-frame capture ===

  1. Run Godot with the --record flag:

     godot --path /path/to/godot-tribute -- --record

  2. PNG frames are saved to:
     - Linux:   ~/.local/share/godot/app_userdata/The Legend of Meile/recording/
     - Windows: %APPDATA%/Godot/app_userdata/The Legend of Meile/recording/
     - macOS:   ~/Library/Application Support/Godot/app_userdata/The Legend of Meile/recording/

  3. Combine with ffmpeg:

     ffmpeg -framerate 30 -i frame_%05d.png -c:v libx264 -crf 18 -pix_fmt yuv420p output.mp4

=== OPTION D: Interactive playback ===

  1. Open Godot Editor
  2. Open this project folder (godot-tribute/)
  3. Press F5 (Play)
  4. Click to start the animation
  5. Use your OS screen recorder to capture

NOTES:
  - Video is 960x540 at 30 FPS
  - Total duration: ~100 seconds
  - The --write-movie method captures both video AND audio
  - Frame capture method (B/C) does NOT capture audio
